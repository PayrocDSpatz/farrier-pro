What we stock

Shoeing supplies, from the nail to the anvil

Here's what's on the shelves in an average week. Stock changes with the season, so if you need a particular size or pattern, call and we'll check before you drive over.

HS

Horseshoes

Steel and aluminum, front and hind, in keg sizes. Sport horse, hunter/jumper and pleasure patterns.

  • Steel
  • Aluminum
  • ACR
  • Fronts & hinds
  • Clipped & unclipped
NL

Nails

Boxed nails by head style and size for every shoe we carry.

  • E-head
  • City head
  • Slim blade
  • Sizes 3–6
PD

Pads & packing

Full and rim pads, wedges, and pour-in and putty packing for sole support.

  • Full pads
  • Rim pads
  • Wedges
  • Pour-in
HT

Hand tools

Rasps, nippers, pull-offs, clinchers, hoof knives, hammers and nail cutters.

  • Rasps
  • Nippers
  • Knives
  • Driving hammers
FG

Forge & anvil

Forges, anvils, stands, tongs, pritchels and fullers for the truck or the shop.

  • Gas forges
  • Anvils
  • Tongs
  • Stands
HC

Hoof care & adhesives

Glue-on adhesives, hoof repair, sole hardeners, thrush treatments and dressings.

  • Adhesives
  • Hoof repair
  • Thrush care
  • Sole hardener
AP

Aprons & gear

Shoeing aprons, gloves, tool boxes and the small parts you go through every week.

  • Aprons
  • Gloves
  • Tool boxes
SO

Special orders

If we don't stock it, ask. We can order specialty and therapeutic shoes, tools and parts.

  • Therapeutic shoes
  • Specialty sizes
