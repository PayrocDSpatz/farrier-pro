The shop

From the nail to the anvil

What's on the shelves in an average week. Stock changes with the season, so call ahead if you need a particular size or pattern.

Horseshoes

Steel and aluminum, fronts and hinds, clipped and unclipped. ACR aluminum on the wall.

Nails

E-head, city head and slim blade, boxed by size for every shoe we carry.

Pads & packing

Full and rim pads, wedges, and pour-in and putty packing for sole support.

Hand tools

Rasps, nippers, pull-offs, clinchers, hoof knives and driving hammers.

Forge & anvil

Gas forges, anvils, stands, tongs, pritchels and fullers.

Hoof care

Glue-on adhesives, hoof repair, sole hardeners and thrush treatments.

Aprons & gear

Leather shoeing aprons, gloves, tool boxes and the small parts you go through.

Special orders

Therapeutic shoes, specialty sizes and anything we don't keep on the shelf.
